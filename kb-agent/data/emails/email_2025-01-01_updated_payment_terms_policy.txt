Effective January 1, 2025, the company has revised its standard customer
payment terms policy. These terms supersede all prior individual agreements

UPDATED STANDARD PAYMENT TERMS:

1. Standard orders: Net 14 days from invoice date (previously Net 30).
2. Bulk orders (above $10,000): Net 21 days from invoice date.
3. Early payment discount is discontinued effective immediately.
4. Late payment penalty: 1.5% per month on outstanding balances beyond
   the due date.
5. All new vendor contracts must reference this policy document.

This update applies to all new invoices issued on or after January 1, 2025.
Any previously issued invoices retain the original agreed terms.
